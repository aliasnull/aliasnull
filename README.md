### Independent Developer · AI & Systems Enthusiast · Open-Source Builder

I explore ideas, build tools, and work on software that pushes beyond conventional limits. My interests span AI-powered developer tools, systems programming, Android and Termux, reverse engineering, game technology, and the web.

## ◈ What Does AliasNull Mean?

**AliasNull = Alias + Null**

- **Alias** represents an identity chosen for oneself: a name beyond the one you're given, defined by what you create and stand for.
- **Null** represents zero, an empty or undefined state. Not an ending, but a clean starting point with no assumptions and no predefined limits.

Together, **AliasNull represents an identity that starts from nothing and is defined by what it builds.** It is about curiosity, independence, and the freedom to explore ideas without being confined to one role, platform, or technology.

## 🧭 Areas I Explore

- **AI & Developer Tools** — AI coding workflows, model-provider integrations, agent capabilities, and tools that make development more accessible.
- **Android & Termux** — Native mobile development environments, command-line tools, build systems, and adapting developer software to Android.
- **Systems & CLI Development** — Terminal applications, Rust, automation, performance, and platform-level experimentation.
- **Reverse Engineering & Research** — Understanding software internals, investigating unfamiliar systems, debugging, and building practical research tools.
- **Game Development & Modding** — Game systems, world simulation, modding, 3D workflows, and interactive experiments.
- **Web Development** — Applications, APIs, backend services, and integrations.

## 🔬 What I'm Working On

My current work includes:

- AI model-provider management and synchronization for coding-agent workflows.
- Native builds and compatibility work that bring modern developer tools to Termux.
- Research into reverse engineering and ways to make technical exploration more effective.
- Mobile-first developer utilities and experiments across software, systems, and game technology.

I share projects publicly when they're ready. Not every experiment or work in progress is intended to be a public release.
